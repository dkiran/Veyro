How it works

No new hardware. Vehicles keep the tracker they already have.

1

Connect

A GPS provider sends us data by API, or a transporter points their existing devices to also send a copy to Veyro. We translate every provider's format into one.

2

Approve

The transporter decides who can see each vehicle, for which trip or contract, and for how long. Access ends automatically, and can be withdrawn at any time.

3

Track

Shippers see every approved vehicle on one live map and one API, whichever GPS provider it uses. If the vehicle is hired by someone else next week, access follows the new approval.

Built around consent

Location data is sensitive. The design starts from who is allowed to see it.

Transporter decides

Nobody sees a vehicle until its owner approves, per shipper and per time window. Approvals expire on their own.

Only what is needed

Shippers see vehicle location and trip status during the approved period, not driver personal details and not history from before or after it.

Every view is logged

Transporters can see who looked at which vehicle and when, and withdraw access at any moment.

Your data stays yours

GPS providers and transporters remain the source of their data. We aggregate it, we do not claim it.

Same call, any provider

You never need to know which GPS platform a truck uses. Ask for a registration number you have been approved for, and get the same format every time. Webhooks and live streaming will notify your systems of trip and delay events.

Questions

Do vehicles need new hardware?

No. Vehicles keep their current tracker. Data reaches us either from the GPS provider or by the transporter adding Veyro as a second destination on the device.

Which GPS providers are connected?

We are onboarding the first pilot providers now. If you are a GPS provider, we would like to hear from you.

Who can see my vehicles?

Only shippers you approve, only for the period you choose. You can see every view and withdraw access whenever you like.

What does it cost?

Pilot partners get early-access terms. Contact us and we will share them.
